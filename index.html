A practical toolkit for everyday student tasks

StudentTools brings commonly needed calculators and study utilities into one browser-based workspace. The goal is to reduce time spent searching for small tools so students can focus on their actual work.

For calculations

Use percentage, grade, GPA, average, discount, fraction, date and unit tools to check repetitive calculations.

For studying

Use the focus timer, notes, word counter and task picker to organize study sessions and written work.

For everyday use

Age, time difference and QR generation are available from the same toolkit without separate software.

Use calculators as study aids

Calculators can handle repetitive work and help check results, but understanding the method behind important school calculations remains useful. For important work, compare results with your own working and your teacher's instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools free?

Yes. The tools are designed to be available through a normal web browser without paid calculator software.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. Notes and preferences can be stored locally in your browser.

Can I use StudentTools on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive for phones, tablets and computers.

Does Study Notes upload my notes?

The notes feature uses your browser's local storage, so saved notes remain in that browser unless site data is cleared.

Can calculators replace learning?

They are study aids and convenient calculators. For schoolwork, understanding the calculation method and checking important results is still recommended.
