ABOUT STUDENTTOOLS

A practical toolkit for everyday student tasks.

StudentTools brings commonly used calculators and study utilities together in one browser-based workspace. The aim is to make small academic and everyday tasks easier without requiring separate software for every job.

🧮

Calculations

Calculate percentages, grades, GPA, averages, fractions, discounts and other mathematical values.

📚

Study Tools

Use the focus timer, notes, word counter, exam countdown and task picker to organize study.

⚙️

Everyday Utilities

Work with dates, times, measurements and QR codes using simple browser-based tools.

Use calculators as study aids

Calculators can save time on repetitive calculations and help check results. However, understanding the method behind an important calculation remains useful, especially for school assignments and examinations. When a result matters, compare it with your own working and the instructions provided by your teacher or school.

Designed to be simple

StudentTools is designed around a straightforward process: choose a tool, enter the required information and view the result. The interface is intended to work on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about StudentTools.

Are StudentTools tools free? +

Yes. The tools are designed to be available through a normal web browser without requiring paid calculator software.

Do I need to create an account? +

No account is required for the main tools. Some preferences and notes can be stored locally in your browser.

Can I use StudentTools on my phone? +

Yes. The website is designed to work across phones, tablets and computers with a modern web browser.

Are my study notes uploaded? +

The Study Notes feature uses your browser's local storage. Your saved notes remain in that browser unless the site's stored data is cleared.

Can calculators replace learning? +

StudentTools is intended as a study aid and convenient calculator. Understanding the method behind important school calculations is still important.

What tools are available? +

StudentTools currently includes percentage, grade, GPA, average, discount, fraction, basic calculator, age, date, unit, word count, focus timer, notes, QR generation, percentage change, time difference, study task selection and exam countdown tools.
