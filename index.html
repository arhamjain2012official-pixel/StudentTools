Free Online Student Tools

StudentTools is a collection of simple online calculators and study utilities designed to make everyday school tasks easier.

Why use online student tools?

Students often need quick calculations while studying, completing assignments, checking marks or planning their time. Small tools can save time and reduce unnecessary calculations.

Useful tools for students

  • Calculate percentages and marks.
  • Check your current age.
  • Count words in an assignment.
  • Convert common units.
  • Plan focused study sessions.
  • Calculate the number of days between dates.

Free and simple

The tools on StudentTools are designed to be straightforward and easy to use. No complicated software is required.
